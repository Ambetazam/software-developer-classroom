## Sistema de pedidos para la Cafetaria de Leandro:

	* La cafeteria de leandro desea organizar la informacion de sus productos, clientes y pedidos.

	* Each customer has a document number, full name. email. phone.

	* For each product save: name, description, prices, quantity available.

	* Customers can make some deliveries, each one has, date, status(delivery not delivery), total of the delivery.

	* A delivery can have many products and a single product can appear in many deliveries. Transitional table: delivery-detail: quantity, and unit price. It is the bill.

	* Each delivery must have associate a payment, that has the payment method of each delivery. date of payment, cost, and description(optional).
